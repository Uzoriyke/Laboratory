The Director

Dr. George O. Anyanwu, Ph.D., M.Sc., BMLS, AIMLS

Medical Laboratory Scientist · Director, Remar Medical Laboratories · Lecturer I, Abia State University

Profile

Two decades of laboratory science, grounded in research

Dr. George O. Anyanwu is a Nigerian Medical Laboratory Scientist with more than 25 years of experience spanning clinical diagnostics, academia, and applied microbiological research. Born in Ubaha Orodo, Imo State, he built his career from the ground up — beginning with his B.Sc. in Medical Laboratory Science at the University of Calabar, and advancing through an M.Sc. and eventually a Ph.D. in Medical Microbiology, both at Imo State University, Owerri.

He founded Remar Medical Laboratories in Aba in 1999, where he has served as Director for over two decades, offering diagnostic testing, research support, and laboratory supplies to the local community. Alongside running the lab, he has taught at the Eastern School of Health Technology, Aba, and currently holds the position of Lecturer I at Abia State University.

His research interests center on the antibacterial and phytochemical properties of medicinal plants, as well as clinical studies on diabetes, thyroid function, and hematological parameters — work reflected in more than ten peer-reviewed publications.
