The scientist behind the lab

Dr. George O. Anyanwu, Ph.D., M.Sc., BMLS, AIMLS

Dr. Anyanwu is a Medical Laboratory Scientist with a Ph.D. in Medical Microbiology from Imo State University. He is the Director of Remar Medical Laboratories and currently serves as a Lecturer I at Abia State University, bringing academic rigour to every diagnosis performed at the lab.

His research — spanning antibacterial plant extracts, diabetes, and clinical hematology — has been published across several peer-reviewed journals, and he holds membership in three professional bodies governing medical laboratory practice in Nigeria.
