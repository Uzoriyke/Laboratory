What we offer

Laboratory services built on scientific rigour

Every test, study, and product that leaves Remar Medical Laboratories is backed by Dr. Anyanwu's academic training and hands-on clinical experience.

01 — Core service

Diagnosis

Remar Medical Laboratories offers comprehensive medical laboratory testing and analysis for individuals, families, and referring clinicians. Samples are handled with strict attention to accuracy, and results are explained in plain language — not just handed over as numbers.

  • ● Full blood count, hematology & lipid profile testing
  • ● Diabetes, thyroid & hormonal panels
  • ● Microbiology & culture-based bacterial testing
  • ● General diagnostic screening & referrals

02 — Applied science

Research

Beyond routine diagnostics, the lab supports advanced scientific research and clinical studies — the same discipline behind Dr. Anyanwu's own peer-reviewed publications on antibacterial plant compounds, diabetes, and hematological parameters.

  • ● Phytochemical & antibacterial extract analysis
  • ● Clinical research support for academic studies
  • ● Collaboration with universities & teaching hospitals

03 — Supply

Sales of Reagents & Equipment

Remar Medical Laboratories also supplies quality laboratory reagents and equipment to fellow practitioners, clinics, and institutions — sourced and handled with the same standards applied to in-house diagnostic work.

  • ● Diagnostic reagents & test kits
  • ● Laboratory glassware & consumables
  • ● Basic diagnostic equipment
